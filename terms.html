1Purpose of the Application

Mathsly is an educational and gamified mental mathematics training application designed to help students and learners improve their calculation speed, mental reflexes, and arithmetic accuracy. The app includes various training modes such as Career Levels, 60s Blitz Mode, Survival Mode, and personalized Weakness Drills, along with brain performance analytics.
