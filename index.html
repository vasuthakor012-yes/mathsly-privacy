1Information We Collect

To provide a personalized mental training experience and track your calculation speed and progress, we collect the following types of information:

A. Personal Information (Account & Profile Data)

  • Google Sign-In Data: When you register or log in using Google Sign-In, we collect your Full Name (Display Name), Email Address, and a unique User Identifier (UID) generated by Firebase Authentication.
  • Demographic Information: To complete your student profile inside the app, we explicitly ask you to provide your Age (between 5 and 100 years) and Gender (Male, Female, or Other).
  • Account Timestamps: We record the date and time when your account is created (createdAt) and when you were last active (lastActive).

B. Gameplay & Brain Analytics Data

As you solve math problems in Career Mode, 60s Blitz Mode, Survival Mode, or Weakness Drills, we automatically calculate and store your performance metrics in Google Cloud Firestore, including:

  • Your current and highest unlocked levels (currentLevel, highestLevel).
  • Total questions solved (totalSolved), mistakes made, and overall accuracy percentage (bestAccuracy, accuracy).
  • Calculation speed metrics, including average speed per question in milliseconds (avgSpeedMs), round time used (roundTimeUsedMs), and Calculations Per Minute (CPM).
  • Operator-specific accuracy and attempt logs for Addition (+), Subtraction (−), Multiplication (×), and Division (÷) to identify your strongest and weakest arithmetic skills.

2Device Permissions We Use

Mathsly requests minimal device permissions strictly necessary for the app to function properly:

INTERNET ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Required to authenticate your account with Google, sync your level progress and brain analytics to our cloud database, and check for the latest app updates.

VIBRATE

Used to provide instant haptic feedback when you tap keys on the in-app numeric keypad during training rounds.

FOREGROUND_SERVICE FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC

Used to ensure smooth background data synchronization of your training sessions with the cloud server.

POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Used to send important app alerts, training reminders, or update notifications.

3How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • To Provide and Maintain the Service: Authenticating your account and saving your level progression across devices.
  • To Generate Brain Diagnostics & Analytics: Analyzing your speed curves and operator accuracy to unlock personalized “Weakness Drills” tailored to your math skills.
  • App Updates & Maintenance: Checking your current app version (versionCode) against our server (app_settings/update_info) to notify you when a new update or bug fix is available.

  • Google Play Services & Google Sign-In: Used for secure, passwordless account authentication.
  • Firebase Authentication: Used to manage user sessions and unique user IDs.
  • Google Cloud Firestore: Used to securely store your student profile, game levels, and session history.

5Data Retention & Account Deletion Rights

You have full control over your personal data and training history. We retain your profile and gameplay statistics only for as long as your account remains active.

How to Delete Your Account and Data

You can permanently delete your account and all associated cloud data directly within the Mathsly app at any time by following these steps:

  1. Open the Mathsly app and go to the Home Dashboard.
  2. Tap the Menu icon (⋮) in the top corner and select ⚙️ Settings.
  3. Tap the “🗑️ DELETE MY ACCOUNT” button and confirm your choice by selecting “DELETE PERMANENTLY”.
Once confirmed, your entire user profile, demographic details, level progress, and session analytics are immediately and permanently wiped from our Firebase Firestore database and Firebase Authentication servers.

6Children’s Privacy

Mathsly is an educational and mental calculation training tool suitable for students and learners of various ages (supporting user profiles aged 5 and above). We collect only limited demographic information (age and gender) alongside Google Sign-In credentials strictly to maintain student profiles and personalize math difficulty levels.

If you are a parent or guardian and you wish to review or delete your child’s account data, you may use the in-app “Delete My Account” feature inside the Settings menu, or contact us directly via email so we can immediately remove the information from our servers.

7Data Security

We value your trust in providing us your personal information. All data transmitted between the Mathsly app and Google Firebase servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS protocols. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.
